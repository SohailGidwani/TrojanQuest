## Branching exploration update

- All new players complete Bovard first. Any accepted edition counts as discovery; trail progress is derived from catches, not a separate database. Then choose libraries (Leavey → Doheny) or outdoors (Alumni Park → McCarthy Quad). Switch whenever desired, resume the next unvisited stop, and revisit any discovered trail place for other time editions. Missing catalogue entries disable the affected trail rather than substituting a destination.
- GPS ownership stays on the Hunt page through celebrations and trail switching. No coordinates are persisted. Background tabs/locked phones may suspend browser GPS; native background geofencing is outside this implementation. Pause/unmount clears the watch. Nearby means a fresh fix within 150 m including uncertainty, with accuracy ≤25 m; collection remains 50 m. The map hides on leaving that radius, stale fixes, or poor accuracy.
- Local demo starts empty under `trojanquest:collection-demo:v2`; prior v1 demo data remains untouched. A simulated new stop starts 200 m away. Only the selected trail ID is remembered separately, under `trojanquest:trail:v1`; this grants no progress and cannot bypass Bovard.
- Demo trail pins use [OpenStreetMap Leavey](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/406923908), [Alumni Park](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/154906676), [McCarthy Quad](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/154906727), and [USC Doheny venue metadata](https://calendar.usc.edu/doheny_memorial_library_dml). OpenStreetMap data © its contributors, [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Other sample places still use illustrative coordinates; live gameplay always uses Cal's published catalogue. Demo pins need entrance calibration on campus before a real launch.
- Real 3D building assets are deferred. Google photorealistic tiles require [billing setup](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/usage-and-billing) and their [usage/attribution rules](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/policies); no paid service or extracted Google Earth asset was added. A later visual layer can use original models or suitably licensed GLB files.
- Verification: 99 tests (1 startup, 85 backend, 13 frontend), type checks, client lint and production build passed. Browser walkthrough completed both trails, switched away/back, resumed after reload, collected a second Bovard time edition, and checked a 390 px layout without horizontal overflow. Browser-injected GPS confirmed a single watch survives trail switching, hides the map for distant/stale/inaccurate fixes, and clears on pause. These are browser simulations, not physical campus or hosted database checks. Review found an edition-revisit regression, fixed and re-reviewed before integration.
